Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add items to my pantry?

You can add items in three ways: tap the "+" button on the Pantry screen to add manually, use the Scanner tab to scan a barcode, or use the Receipt Scanner to import items from a grocery receipt photo.

How does barcode scanning work?

Point your camera at a product barcode in the Scanner tab. The app looks up the product in multiple databases (UPCitemdb, Open Food Facts) and auto-fills the item name and category. If the lookup fails, you can use the AI photo scanner or enter details manually.

How do AI-generated recipes work?

Go to the Recipes tab and tap "Generate Recipe." The AI analyzes your current pantry items and dietary preferences to suggest recipes you can make with what you already have. You can filter by cuisine type and dietary restrictions.

How do I set up expiry alerts?

When adding an item, set an expiration date. Go to Profile > Expiry Alerts to configure how many days before expiry you'd like to be notified. The app will alert you when items are approaching their expiration date.

How do loyalty cards work?

Go to Profile > Loyalty Cards (or tap the card icon on the Pantry screen). Add your store loyalty cards with the card number and barcode format. At checkout, tap a card to show the full-screen barcode for the cashier to scan.

What are storage locations?

Storage locations (Fridge, Freezer, Pantry Shelf, Counter) help you organize where items are physically stored. You can filter your pantry by location and add custom locations in Profile > Storage Locations.

Is my data stored locally or in the cloud?

By default, all your data is stored locally on your device. If you enable household sharing, pantry data syncs securely to our servers so household members can share a pantry.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete all local data by uninstalling the app or clearing app data in your device settings. For server-stored data (household accounts), email us at support@pantrymanager.app to request account deletion.

The barcode scanner isn't recognizing my product.

Not all barcodes are in public databases. If a lookup fails, you can: (1) use the AI photo scanner to identify the product visually, (2) enter the details manually, or (3) save a correction so future scans of the same barcode auto-fill correctly.

How do I report a bug?

Email us at support@pantrymanager.app with a description of the issue, your device model, and iOS/Android version. Screenshots are helpful!

System Requirements

  • iOS 16.0 or later
  • Android 10 or later
  • Camera access required for barcode scanning and receipt scanning
  • Internet connection required for AI recipe generation, barcode lookups, and price comparison
