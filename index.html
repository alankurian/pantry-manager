Privacy Policy

Pantry Manager — Last updated: March 11, 2026

Pantry Manager ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Pantry items — Names, quantities, categories, expiration dates, and storage locations of food items you add to the app.
  • Loyalty cards — Store names, card numbers, and barcode data you choose to save.
  • Dietary preferences — Dietary restrictions and preferences you configure (e.g., vegetarian, gluten-free).
  • Health data — Weight, height, age, and activity level if you use the calorie tracking feature. This data is stored locally on your device.
  • Household information — If you create or join a household, your display name and household membership.
  • Zipcode — If you use the price comparison feature, your zipcode is used to estimate distances to nearby stores.

Information collected automatically:

  • Camera data — When you use the barcode scanner or photo scanning features, camera access is used in real-time to detect barcodes and food items. Images are processed and are not stored permanently.
  • Receipt images — When you scan a receipt, the image is sent to our server for AI-powered text extraction. The image is processed in memory and is not retained after extraction is complete.
  • Crash and usage data — Minimal, anonymous crash reports to help us improve app stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the app's core functionality (pantry tracking, recipe generation, shopping lists).
  • To generate personalized recipes based on your pantry contents and dietary preferences using AI.
  • To look up product information from barcode databases (UPCitemdb, Open Food Facts) when you scan items.
  • To estimate grocery prices and store distances when you use the price comparison feature.
  • To sync pantry data across household members if you opt into household sharing.
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs through anonymous crash reporting.

3. Data Storage and Security

Local storage: The majority of your data (pantry items, loyalty cards, dietary preferences, health data, barcode corrections, cached lookups) is stored locally on your device using encrypted storage. This data never leaves your device unless you enable household sharing.

Server storage: If you use household sharing or user authentication features, your pantry data and account information are stored on secure servers with encryption at rest and in transit (TLS 1.3).

AI processing: When generating recipes or scanning photos/receipts, data is sent to our server for AI processing. This data is used only for the immediate request and is not stored or used for training purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

The app may interact with the following third-party services:

  • UPCitemdb — For barcode product lookups. Only the barcode number is sent. UPCitemdb Privacy Policy
  • Open Food Facts — For barcode product lookups. Only the barcode number is sent. Open Food Facts Privacy Policy
  • Google Favicon API — To fetch store logos for loyalty cards. Only the store domain name is sent.

5. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect advertising identifiers or tracking data.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not collect your contacts, browsing history, or financial information.
  • We do not use your data for targeted advertising.

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • Delete your data — You can delete all app data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing app data in your device settings. For server-stored data (household accounts), contact us to request deletion.
  • Camera access — You can revoke camera permissions at any time through your device settings. The barcode scanner and photo features will be unavailable without camera access.
  • Location data — Zipcode entry is optional and only used for price comparison. You can skip or remove it at any time.
  • Household sharing — You can leave a household at any time, which stops syncing your data with other members.

7. Children's Privacy

Pantry Manager is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
